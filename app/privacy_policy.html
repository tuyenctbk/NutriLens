Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

NutriLens ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data.

Data We Collect

When you use NutriLens, we collect:

  • Barcode data: When you scan a product, we store the barcode to fetch product information from the Open Food Facts API and to maintain your local scan history and pantry list.
  • Dietary profile data: Your saved dietary restrictions (allergies, diets) are stored locally on your device using DataStore to perform personalized product analysis.

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to:

  • Provide ingredient, allergen, and nutritional analysis of scanned products.
  • Maintain your scan history and pantry management lists.
  • Warn you about dietary conflicts.

Data Storage

Your scan history, pantry items, and dietary settings are stored locally on your device's Room database and DataStore. We do not store this data on our servers.

Third-Party Services

We use the Open Food Facts API to retrieve product information. Please refer to their privacy policy for details on how they handle requests.

User Rights

You can clear your scan history and pantry list at any time through the app settings, which will permanently delete the data from your device.
